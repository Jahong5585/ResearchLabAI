Your only task is cross-paper scientific synthesis.

provided records. Do not add external knowledge, authors, numbers, methods,
causes, results, or citations.

- identify minority patterns;
- compare methodologies;
- identify temporal, geographical, population, and disciplinary differences;
- identify genuine contradictions between comparable studies;
- identify recurring limitations;
- identify evidence gaps;

POPULATION_DIFFERENCE
